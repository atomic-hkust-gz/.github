## About Us

At **ATOMIC Lab**, we design **distributed intelligent systems** that bring together microelectronics, low-power wireless networking, sensing, and autonomous robotics.

Our vision is an **AI-enabled Internet of Micro-Things**: large numbers of resource-constrained devices and robots, across a range of sizes, that can **sense, communicate, and collaborate** in complex physical environments.

We work across the full stack—from circuits and wireless protocols to sensing algorithms, edge intelligence, and multi-robot coordination. Our research follows a **circuit–system–algorithm co-design** philosophy, with an emphasis on real hardware prototypes, integrated systems, and experimental validation.

## Research Directions

| Area | What we explore |
| --- | --- |
| **Micro-Integrated & Crystal-Free Systems** | Miniaturized, ultra-low-power platforms and techniques for operating reliably without crystal oscillators. |
| **Low-Power Wireless Networking** | Reliable, synchronized, and energy-efficient networking for dense and mobile systems, including TSCH, BLE, LoRa, SLE, and HaLow. |
| **Infrastructure-Less Tracking & Localization** | Scalable tracking and localization with minimal dependence on fixed anchors or other deployed infrastructure. |
| **Distributed Sensing & Sensor Fusion** | Combining sensing modalities and distributed observations for robust, resource-aware perception. |
| **Embedded & Edge Intelligence** | Intelligent services on constrained devices, including efficient LLM inference, shared context, and multi-agent collaboration. |
| **Swarm Robotics & Cooperative Intelligence** | Scalable coordination among robots through local sensing, wireless communication, and distributed decision-making. |
| **Miniature Tracking & Embedded Systems** | Compact sensing, localization, and embedded computing platforms for physical-world applications. |

Explore our work in more detail on the **[Research page](https://atomic.hkust-gz.edu.cn/research/)**.
